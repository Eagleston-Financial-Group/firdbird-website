# Insurance agency visitor experience

Research reviewed on November 8, 2026:

- https://www.chubb.com/us-en/ : audience-based coverage navigation and visible client-service paths.
- https://www.hubinternational.com/en-us/solutions/personal-insurance/ : advisor-led entry points and clear coverage categories.
- https://www.marsh.com/en/services/marsh-mclennan-agency.html : industry-focused advice and local client relationships.

Applied those navigation principles to Firebird's existing services. Added business, home/family, and existing-client entry paths immediately below the hero. Organized the coverage area around construction, practices, and properties, with secondary business, trucking, and personal links. Updated desktop navigation to Business insurance, Personal insurance, Industries, Client services, and Our agency. The existing mobile menu continues to expose each coverage category. Expanded the client-service panel for certificates, policy changes, claims, and payments.

Preserved the approved copper #A44520, original logo, desert background, quote forms, and existing coverage routes. No reviews, ratings, prices, savings claims, or partner relationships were invented or taken from other companies.

Validation: build prerendered 69 routes. All internal route and fragment links in the rendered pages resolved. Browser checks at 390, 768, 1100, and 1440 pixels showed no horizontal overflow. Industry navigation and the existing-client entry path were exercised. No browser warnings or errors were recorded. Local site remains on port 3001; no production deployment or API change.
